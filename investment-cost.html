Cake Store Franchise Cost & Investment

Understand the financial blueprint, shop fit-out expenditures, display chillers, and initial working capital required to establish a thriving retail cake shop.

Investment Estimate by Cost Component

The initial capital required to launch a standard 200–500 sq.ft. cake shop outlet typically ranges between ₹10 Lakhs to ₹18 Lakhs depending on city tier and market footfall.

Component Estimated Bracket Description
Shop Fit-out & Interior Décor ₹4.0L – ₹6.0L Standardized flooring, branded front signage, ambient lighting & counter fixtures.
Refrigerated Display Chillers ₹2.5L – ₹3.5L Commercial curved-glass cake chillers and temperature-controlled display units.
Initial Opening Inventory ₹1.5L – ₹2.5L Fresh celebration cakes, pastries, packaged cookies, savories, and beverages.
Billing POS & Peripherals ₹60K – ₹1.0L Touch POS system, barcode scanner, thermal printer & inventory software.
Working Capital Buffer ₹1.0L – ₹2.0L Initial operational liquidity for staff expenses, utilities, and daily operations.
Total Estimated Setup Budget ₹10L – ₹18L Turnkey store setup ready for daily customer operations.

Space & Technical Specifications

  • Carpet Area: Minimum 200 to 500 sq.ft. ground-floor retail shop.
  • Frontage Width: 10 to 12 feet wide glass frontage for clear cake display showcase.
  • Electrical Connection: 3-Phase commercial meter (8 KW to 10 KW load capacity) to support chillers and AC.
  • Preferred Zones: High-street markets, prominent road corners, dense residential townships, and hospital/school hubs.
