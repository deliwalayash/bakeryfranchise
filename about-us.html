Empowering Retail Bakery Entrepreneurs

Bakery Franchise Partner is India's dedicated franchise discovery, location feasibility, and turnkey store setup advisory portal for retail cake shops and confectionery businesses.

Who We Are

Bakery Franchise Partner was established to simplify the process of opening and operating profitable retail cake shops in India. The retail bakery industry is experiencing unprecedented growth driven by year-round celebration demand, impulse snacking, and changing urban consumer lifestyles.

Many aspiring business owners and commercial property holders want to enter this lucrative segment but face uncertainty regarding shop carpet area requirements, refrigeration equipment sourcing, initial inventory planning, and staff training. We bridge this gap by providing transparent, structured feasibility consulting and end-to-end guidance.

We operate as an independent facilitator committed to high business ethics, zero upfront online fees, and genuine partner support.

Our Advisory Services

How We Support Your Store Launch

We guide you through every stage of establishing your retail cake shop:

1. Location & Space Audit

We review your proposed shop premises (200–500 sq.ft.), evaluating glass frontage, pedestrian footfall, and catchment demographics.

2. 3D Layout & Blueprint

Assistance with standardized architectural blueprints maximizing customer movement corridors and premium display visibility.

3. Equipment & Chillers

Vendor sourcing for commercial curved-glass display chillers, ambient bakery racks, touch POS billing terminals, and signage.

4. Staff Training & Operations

Structured hospitality training for counter staff covering customer service, cake message piping, hygiene protocols, and POS billing.
