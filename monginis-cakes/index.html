Essential Overview

Bakery Franchise Opportunity

Key considerations for entrepreneurs looking to start a retail cake shop or bakery outlet.

Store & Space Considerations

  • Commercial Location: High-density residential zones, main commercial markets, or near educational/office hubs.
  • Carpet Area: Typical cake shop formats generally require 200 sq.ft. to 500 sq.ft. with prominent frontage (subject to specific brand standards).
  • Frontage & Visibility: Minimum 10–12 ft wide glass frontage for refrigerated cake display visibility.
  • Utilities: Dedicated electricity connection for refrigeration units, air conditioning, and water supply.

Investment & Commercial Terms

  • Fit-out & Equipment: Display chillers, counter fixtures, branding signage, and point-of-sale setup.
  • Initial Stock & Inventory: Fresh cakes, savories, packaged confectionery, and beverages.
  • Working Capital: Operational buffer for daily staffing, utility charges, and local store management.
  • Transparency Note: Investment and eligibility details are discussed based on the specific franchise opportunity and location.
