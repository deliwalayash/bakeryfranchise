Privacy Policy

Platform Disclosure:

Bakery Franchise Partner (bakeryfranchisepartner.in) operates as an independent franchise enquiry, space feasibility, and consulting facilitation platform. Personal details submitted are utilized solely for franchise communication and store setup evaluation.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit our website or submit an enquiry through our lead form, we may collect the following personal and commercial information provided voluntarily by you:

  • Personal Identifiers: Full Name.
  • Contact Information: Mobile Phone Number and Email Address.
  • Location Details: City, Town, State, and Preferred Retail Locality/Area.
  • Franchise Preferences: Target format (retail cake store, bakery kiosk, or cafe format), approximate investment budget, and commercial property details.
  • Technical Information: IP address, device type, browser information, and referral URLs collected via standard web analytics and Google Ads tracking tags.

2. Purpose of Information Collection

The information collected is used for lawful business purposes, including:

  • Processing, evaluating, and responding to your cake shop franchise enquiry.
  • Contacting you via telephone calls or email regarding cake shop franchise opportunities, setup costs, space requirements, and onboarding steps.
  • Evaluating location viability and commercial property suitability.
  • Measuring advertising performance, website functionality, and lead quality.

3. How We Share and Protect Your Data

We take data privacy seriously and adhere to the following principles:

  • No Unauthorized Selling: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to unassociated third-party telemarketers.
  • Data Security: We implement industry-standard technical measures (such as SSL encryption) to protect your submitted information against unauthorized access or alteration.

4. Google Ads, Analytics & Cookies

Our website may use cookies and conversion tracking tags (including Google Ads and Google Analytics) to understand visitor interactions and optimize ad campaigns. These tools collect anonymized usage data. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser settings.
