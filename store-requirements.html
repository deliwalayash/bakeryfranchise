Store Space & Location Guidelines

Key technical specifications, carpet area requirements, frontage standards, and high-footfall location criteria needed to establish a thriving retail bakery shop.

Essential Store Space Specifications

Selecting the right retail footprint ensures optimal display of cakes, comfortable customer walk-in movement, and smooth daily billing flow.

Parameter Requirement Operational Significance
Carpet Area 200 – 500 Sq.Ft. Accommodates curved display chillers, dry bakery racks, and customer counter.
Frontage Width 10 – 12 Feet Min Clear full-glass visibility for showcase cakes and branded facade signage.
Floor Level Ground Floor Direct street access with zero or minimal step obstruction for foot-traffic ease.
Power Load 3-Phase (8–10 KW) Essential for running commercial display chillers, air-conditioning, and POS.
Water & Drainage Dedicated Line Necessary for daily retail sanitization, utensil washing, and counter hygiene.
Lease Tenure 3 to 5 Years Registered commercial lease deed ensuring business stability and steady returns.

Recommended Location Types

Bakery products depend strongly on impulsive buying and neighborhood foot traffic:

High-Street Retail Markets

Main market roads, corner shops, and commercial bazaars with sustained pedestrian footfall and evening crowd density.

Dense Residential Hubs

Surrounding large residential apartment complexes, family neighborhoods, schools, and birthday/celebration communities.
